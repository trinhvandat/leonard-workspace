Rough notes while I use this on a real task. One model drafts; another grades the draft
against an explicit rubric; you feed the critique back and redraft. Stop when the score
plateaus, not when you run out of patience.

## What makes it work

The rubric is the whole game. A vague "is this good?" evaluator just adds latency; a
specific rubric — "cites a source for every claim, no sentence over 25 words" — gives the
optimizer something to climb. This is the loop [[agentic-workflow-patterns]] says to save
for when quality is actually measurable.

## Open questions

- How many rounds before the gains are noise? Early guess: two or three.
- Does the same model grading its own work cheat? Suspect yes — a separate grader helps.
