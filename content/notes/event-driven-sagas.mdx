Rough notes, not ready for anyone but me yet. A saga is how you get a multi-step business
transaction across services when a real distributed transaction isn't on the table — and
how you undo it when step four fails.

## Orchestration vs choreography

- **Orchestration**: one coordinator tells each service what to do next. Easy to reason
  about, one place to look, one place to become a bottleneck.
- **Choreography**: services react to each other's events with no central brain. Loosely
  coupled, wonderful until you need to answer "why did this order get stuck" and there's no
  single log to read.

## Compensating actions

The hard half isn't the happy path, it's the undo: every step needs a compensating action
that semantically reverses it, because you can't roll back a charge you already made —
you can only refund it. This is the bill that the [[database-per-service-tax]] warned was
coming.
